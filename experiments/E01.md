# E01 — Transaction-only vs Graph-aware Risk Detection

## Question

Does graph context improve coordinated fraud-ring detection when transaction-level features overlap legitimate traffic?

## Train World

* 10,000 legitimate transactions
* 5 fraud rings
* 8 accounts per ring
* 4 transactions per account
* Seed: 42

## Test World

* 5,000 legitimate transactions
* 3 unseen fraud rings
* 8 accounts per ring
* 4 transactions per account
* Seed: 1337

## Models

### Baseline

Features:

* amount
* account age

### Hybrid

Features:

* amount
* account age
* customer degree
* card degree
* device degree
* IP degree
* merchant degree
* component size

## Results

| Model | Precision | Recall | F1 | PR-AUC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction baseline | 0.0191 | 0.4896 | 0.0368 | 0.0190 |
| Graph-aware hybrid | 0.2207 | 1.0000 | 0.3616 | 0.9551 |
